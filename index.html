Housing · Glasgow

Glasgow City Council – Homelessness Team

If you are homeless or likely to become homeless, contact the council and say you need to make a homelessness application. The council can assess your situation and arrange temporary accommodation if required.

Our goal is a simple place to explore support options and contact providers directly.

About Family Backup

Built around what families actually need.

Family Backup is an early-stage project being developed to help families find relevant local support and community services, starting in Glasgow.

We're listening first. The survey will help us understand which challenges matter most and what information families want in one easy-to-use place.

Our promise

We will aim to share clear, useful information and direct families to the organisations that provide support. Family Backup is not an emergency service and does not replace professional advice.
